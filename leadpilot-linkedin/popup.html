          LinkedIn does not show email addresses in search results, and LeadPilot never opens “Contact info” or reads emails from pages. The compliant way is LinkedIn’s own data export: Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections. When LinkedIn sends you the download link, unzip it and choose Connections.csv here. It holds an email only for connections who allowed their connections to see it.

          If this window closes when you pick a file, press ↗ at the top to open LeadPilot in a browser tab, or paste the file’s content below.

          Leads are matched by profile URL, or by exact name + the same company when exactly one lead fits. An email you already have is never overwritten. Use emails in line with the person’s consent and the law that applies to you (for example GDPR or India’s DPDP Act), and always give an easy way to opt out.

              Post publishing

              Off by default. When off, a due post just waits for you to press Publish.

              Diagnostics

              Something not working? Press Run diagnostics, then Copy report and paste it to whoever is helping you. The report describes the structure of the attached LinkedIn page and the extension’s state. Page text, names and message bodies are replaced by lengths or placeholders.

              Data

              Everything is stored locally in this browser (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is uploaded.
